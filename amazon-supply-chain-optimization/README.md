## Overview

E-commerce growth puts steady pressure on supply chains. This project reviews **Amazon's procurement, transportation, warehousing, and distribution processes** and proposes an information systems solution that improves efficiency, reduces cost, adds sustainability, and builds resilience.

## Project Objectives

| Objective | Focus |
|:--|:--|
| **Efficiency** | Streamline procurement, optimize transportation routes, and speed up order fulfillment |
| **Cost reduction** | Cost-effective procurement, vendor management, and less excess inventory through demand forecasting |
| **Sustainability** | Eco-friendly packaging, renewable energy, and responsible sourcing |
| **Technology** | Automation, real-time tracking, predictive maintenance, and blockchain traceability |
| **Risk management** | Contingency plans for disruptions and real-time monitoring |
| **Collaboration** | Better communication across supply chain nodes and partners |

## What the Proposal Covers

- **Problem definition and research questions** on current inefficiencies, technology use, environmental impact, and resilience.
- **Proposed solution** with an analysis of its strengths, weaknesses, reliability, scalability, and security.
- **Business areas affected and key components:** software, hardware, database, network, and business process re-engineering.
- **Cost-benefit analysis:** tangible benefits (cost savings, efficiency, risk mitigation) and intangible ones (customer satisfaction, brand reputation).
- **Implementation methodology:** agile delivery, prototyping, parallel implementation, continuous integration and testing, and user training.
- **A 20-month project plan** across system selection and implementation, with milestones, deliverables, and KPIs.
- **Project team roles:** project manager, business analyst, IT lead, supply chain experts, change management specialist, training coordinator, and QA analyst.
- **Limitations and future work.**
